Master the beautiful art of Quran recitation with proper pronunciation, Tajweed, and spiritual understanding. This comprehensive course takes you from the basics to fluent Quran reading.

About This Course

The Qaida O Nazra Course is designed for students who want to learn the fundamentals of Quran recitation. This course covers Arabic alphabets, pronunciation rules, and basic Tajweed principles.

Our experienced instructors will guide you through each lesson with patience and dedication, ensuring you develop a strong foundation in Quran reading.

What You'll Learn

Arabic Alphabets

Learn all 28 Arabic letters with proper pronunciation

Pronunciation

Master correct pronunciation of Arabic sounds

Basic Tajweed

Understand fundamental Tajweed rules and principles

Quran Reading

Read Quran verses with proper recitation

Course Timeline

This course is designed to be completed at your own pace, with regular assessments to track your progress.

  • Week 1-2: Arabic Alphabet Recognition
  • Week 3-4: Basic Pronunciation Rules
  • Week 5-8: Joining Letters and Words
  • Week 9-12: Introduction to Tajweed
  • Week 13-16: Quran Reading Practice
